Playful geometric: cream background, bold primaries, circles/squares/stars that pop and morph with springy easing, round geometric type, marimba groove.

Use when: consumer apps, kids/education, social teasers, friendly product launches, event announcements.
Signature moves: `pop_in(t, ease="spring:wobbly")` on beats; `Morph([circle, square, star])`; `Grid(..., order="center")` of dots; `ShapeWipe(color_="accent")`; stickers (`Chip`) and `Button.press`; shapes bleeding off-frame behind copy.
Avoid: grain/glitch textures, thin type, more than 4 colours on screen.
Fonts: URW Gothic and Lato (free, often preinstalled on Linux). Better: Unbounded, Nunito or Manrope (free, Cyrillic).
