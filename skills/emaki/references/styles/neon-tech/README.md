Neon tech: dark grid world, light writes the words and draws the connectors, heavy caps slam on the beat over pink flash frames, perspective panels, whips and scale-throughs, bloom.

Use when: AI/tech product reels, launches, "how it works" explainers with energy, creator-style hooks.
Signature moves: `Text.write_on(t, head=True)`; `KineticWords(words, beats, flashes=["flash", "bg"])`; `Comet` along connectors between `Card`s; `GridPlane(rx=72)` floor + `camera.glide`/`travel` (never wobble); panels `tilt_in(t, ry=-28)`; `Whip`/`ScaleThrough`/`FlashCut`; `.echo()` on slams; `.smear()` on fast moves.
The dynamics (dynamics block) carry over to calm or light looks: keep the grammar, change the look.
Avoid: slow fades, long holds, more than one accent hue.
Fonts: Lato Black (free, often preinstalled on Linux). Better: Unbounded or Druk Wide Cyrillic for slams, JetBrains Mono for labels.
