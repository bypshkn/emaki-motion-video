# Pixabay: terms that apply to a client film (summary)

Pages read on 2026-10-19. This is our own summary, not the binding text and not legal advice.
The binding text is always the current one on Pixabay's site:

- Content License (binding): https://pixabay.com/service/terms/
- License summary: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
- FAQ, music section (Content ID): https://pixabay.com/service/faq/

## What the license allows

- Free use of photos, video, music and sound effects downloaded from pixabay.com.
- No credit to the author is required.
- Changing the file and building it into a new work, including client videos, ads and
  promotional films that are sold or published.

## What it does not allow

- Selling or sharing the file on its own, in substantially the same form as on the site. In a
  film the file must be part of a larger creative work.
- Using a file that shows recognisable trademarks, logos or brands commercially in relation to
  goods or services.
- Immoral, illegal, misleading or deceptive use, especially with recognisable people.
- Using the file as part of a trademark, business name or service mark.

Some files may carry other rights (trademarks, people's privacy, property). Checking whether a
third party's consent is needed is the user's responsibility.

## Music and Content ID

- Some authors register their tracks in YouTube Content ID; the track page shows a shield icon.
  Such tracks are still free to use, but uploads may get automated claims.
- A Content ID claim is not a copyright strike: it can affect monetisation or visibility, not
  the channel's standing.
- To dispute a claim: in YouTube Studio choose that you have a license, and give the track
  title, the track URL on Pixabay, the license link and how the music is used. A track with the
  shield has a license certificate in its Download menu: keep it.
- A second-level appeal only when sure: Pixabay warns that a rejected appeal can escalate to a
  strike.
- Other platforms (Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest) use their own detection; dispute with the
  same data, and the platform may contact info@pixabay.com.
- Pixabay advises keeping records of every download (file name, page, screenshots), downloading
  only from pixabay.com, and avoiding music-only uploads.
