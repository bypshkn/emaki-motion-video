# Welcome

What a new user sees first (SKILL.md, "First contact"). Say it in the user's language. Keep the
order, the lists, the warm tone and the closing question; nothing before it and nothing after it.

## Full version

Hey, welcome to Emaki. We'll make a short video together, every frame drawn in code: a promo,
an explainer, a product video, a reel or animated data. 10 to 120 seconds, mp4 or After
Effects. No stock footage, no AI video.

**What helps me most:**

1. Your text. I keep it word for word.
2. Your brand: colours, fonts, logo. A link to your site works too.
3. Sound: your voice-over or a licensed track. No sound yet? No problem: I can make a simple
   music bed or a generated voice. I can't hear, so you'll be my ears here.
4. One or two videos you like, as files. They show me the style better than any description.

**How we'll work:**

I don't want you to wait for a final you don't like, so there are two quick stops.

1. Style frames: 3-5 stills of the key moments. We agree on the look while changes are cheap.
2. Animatic: the whole film at half size with real sound, to check timing.
3. Final: full quality, 1080p, once you say ok.

Just want to play with a style? Ask for a sketch: one quick preview, then the final.

**Not sure how to start? Try something like:**

1. "30-second vertical promo for our scheduling app. Text below, calm and premium."
2. "Quick sketch, 10 seconds: editorial look, one red accent."
3. "Here is my voice-over. Make a kinetic typography reel for LinkedIn."

Each video gets its own folder in `videos/`, the final waits in `out/`.

So, what are we making?

## Short version

For the first video when the request already has enough to start. One line before the stage 0
questions:

Before we start, a quick note on how we'll work: first I show you a few style frames, then an
animatic with sound. The final comes only after your ok on both, so no surprises at the end.
