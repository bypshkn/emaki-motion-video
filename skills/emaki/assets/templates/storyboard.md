# Storyboard

Format: __ASPECT__, __DURATION__ s, __FPS__ fps. BPM: __BPM__ (bar = __BAR__ s).
Copy marked [DEMO COPY] was written by Claude; everything else is verbatim from the brief.

## Motion concept (five lines, before any design)

- Carrier: the object that lives through the whole film
- Chain: idea 1 -> idea 2 -> ... (one arrow per transformation; no fade, no cut to empty)
- Camera: its path through the whole piece
- Signature moments: two or three, with times
- Rhythm: BPM and how often something hits

## Light (art_direction.md 5b)

Where the light comes from, what it falls on, where the accent lives, what has material
(soft shadow, glass, sheen), what is in focus when.
